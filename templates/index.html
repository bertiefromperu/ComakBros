Our Story

Two brothers, one philosophy: do it right

In 1978, Bruce Comak and his brother Barry — new college graduates — started a partnership that has grown for decades. They built their reputation by strictly adhering to a philosophy of quality work, attention to detail, and service above and beyond customer expectations.

From the initial consultation, through design and development, to construction and implementation, Comak Bros. manages every project personally to ensure satisfaction, quality, and integrity in all aspects of the work — a commitment that continues today, decades later.
